# Discovery System

Discovery answers one question about every viewer and every thing: *has this viewer found this yet?* It is the epistemic layer of Esiana — the machinery of dramatic irony, formalized. The GM knows the Gray Cartel runs the docks; the party does not; the wiki serves both truths at once without maintaining two wikis.

Three mix-ups cause nearly all discovery confusion, so separate them firmly. Visibility is permission: who may read. Editorial status is commentary: where the GM says this stands. Discovery is knowledge: whether the party has learned it exists. A page needs all three to go right before a player sees it, and each is managed in a different place for a reason — permission is set once and rarely changes, commentary evolves with the story, knowledge flips at table beats.

In practice the system behaves as one rule applied everywhere: browsing, search, backlinks, link suggestions, map lists, and party-knowledge views all omit what the viewer has not discovered. There are no side channels. A hidden faction does not appear in search results, does not render as a tempting broken link when mentioned, and does not show its pins on the party's map. Count-only summaries may acknowledge that *something* exists in a category without saying what, which reads as honesty rather than a tease.

For GMs, the operational consequences are few but absolute. Write secrets early and hide them — hidden preparation is the intended workflow, not a hack. Reveal at the beat where the party learns, knowing the reveal propagates through every surface at once. Preview as the player whenever leakage is conceivable. And remember that discovery hides but never protects: a hidden page with the wrong visibility is one shared link away from exposure, because discovery governs finding, not reading.

The companion feature guide covers the daily workflow — preparing hidden, revealing on discovery, tracking beliefs through lore claims and rumors. This page is the why behind it: one question, asked uniformly, so secrets stay secret exactly until they should not.
